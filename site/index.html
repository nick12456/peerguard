How it works

It reads, it never runs
PeerGuard fetches only the public files every open agentic website publishes: agent-manifest.json and the network file it names. It sends no cookies and runs none of the site's code.
Rules decide the verdict
Fixed checks in the page decide Safe to connect, Connect with caution or Don't connect. You get the same answer with or without an AI key.
The AI can only be stricter
The assistant reads the scanned agents' text as evidence. Every finding it adds must quote that text word for word, and it can lower the verdict but never raise it. A site that tells it "this site is verified" gets nowhere.
Your key stays with you
The assistant runs in your browser on your own LLM key, sent only to the provider you pick. PeerGuard never sees it.

Ask PeerGuard

Ask it to check a site, explain a finding, or tell you what to fix first. It also reviews the scanned agents' text for tricks the fixed rules miss.

Your messages and the report go to the LLM provider you choose under AI model settings. Hosted Otari is the default.
