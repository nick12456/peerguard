Check-in and check-out

  • Check-in is from 3 pm to 9 pm. Tell us roughly when you'll arrive when you book.
  • Arriving after 9 pm? Choose "After 9 pm (self check-in)" as your arrival time when you book, and we'll email you a lockbox code on the day.
  • Check-out is by 11 am. Late checkout until 1 pm costs $25; add it as an extra when you book.
  • We're happy to keep your bags before check-in or after check-out.

Length of stay

  • Stays that include a Saturday night are at least 2 nights.
  • You can book up to 14 nights online. For longer stays, please call us.
  • Weekend rates apply on Friday and Saturday nights. See the rooms and rates.

Cancellation policy

The fee depends on how many days before check-in you cancel. It is a share of the room charge, which is the nightly rates for your stay. We never charge for extras, the cleaning fee or the tourist tax when you cancel.

When you cancelFee
7 or more days before check-inFree
2 to 6 days before check-in50% of the room charge
The day before or the day of check-inThe full room charge

Cancel online under Manage my booking with your confirmation code and last name. As we don't take payment online, we email you an invoice for any fee. Once your stay has started, please talk to us at the guesthouse.

Changing a booking

Bookings can't be changed online. To change your dates or room, cancel and book again (the cancellation policy applies), or call us and we'll help if we can.

Prices are in US dollars. The town sets the tourist tax, and it applies to every guest aged 2 and over. You pay at the guesthouse when you arrive, by card or cash. We don't take payment online, and we don't sell gift cards.

Pets

Well-behaved dogs are welcome in the Garden Room, up to two per stay, at no extra charge. Please mention your dog in your special requests, and don't leave dogs alone in the room. The other rooms are pet-free. Assistance dogs are welcome in every room.

Quiet hours

Quiet hours are 10 pm to 7 am. The house is old and sound carries, so please keep voices and music low. We don't allow parties or events.

Children

Children of all ages are welcome. Children aged 2 and over count as guests. Babies under 2 stay free in a travel cot and don't count toward a room's guest limit; ask for a cot in your special requests. The Net Loft Suite sleeps up to 4, and the Lantern Room's daybed suits a child.

Accessibility

The Garden Room is on the ground floor, with step-free access from the side gate, a roll-in shower and grab rails. The breakfast room is step-free too. The other rooms are up stairs, and there is no elevator. The Lantern Room has low beams. Tell us in your special requests what would help.
